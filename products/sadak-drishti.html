SadakDrishti (सड़क दृष्टि)
Autonomous AI Road Health Inspection

Transforming everyday vehicle dashcams, public buses, and drone footage into an automated, real-time road condition survey network. Detect potholes, cracks, and surface distress before accidents happen.

Pavement Intelligence

Automated Road Anomaly Classification

Trained on hundreds of thousands of kilometers of diverse national highways, state corridors, and municipal streets.

Pothole Metric Estimation

Identifies potholes with 99.1% precision, computing estimated surface area, volume, and depth severity to immediately flag hazardous road craters for emergency patching.

Crack & Fatigue Analysis

Differentiates between superficial hairline fissures, longitudinal joints, and dangerous load-related alligator cracking requiring structural base asphalt restoration.

Road Markings & Furniture

Audits lane line reflectivity, pedestrian zebra crossing degradation, missing speed breakers, and obscured traffic signs to preserve overall corridor safety.

Deployment Architecture

Zero Friction Edge Deployment

SadakDrishti turns any moving fleet into a continuous surveying force without requiring dedicated high-cost laser vehicles.

Deploy on Patrol Fleets or Existing Municipal Vehicles

Rather than commissioning costly annual surveying contracts that take months, SadakDrishti runs passively in the background on garbage collection trucks, highway patrol cars, and public transit buses.

Edge Box (NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano / Nano): Real-time inference at 60 FPS directly inside the vehicle with zero cellular bandwidth overhead.
Smartphone / Dashcam SDK: Plug-and-play Android and iOS application that utilizes on-device NPU hardware for highway inspections.
Automated GIS Work Order Sync: Automatically emits geo-tagged GeoJSON/Shapefiles directly into municipal enterprise GIS systems and PWD portals.
